# 5 — Communication

Everything in IE Central for talking to people — inside the company and outside it. This section
covers the built-in **email** client, **messages and chat**, company **announcements**, video
**meetings** with automatic AI notes, **Zoom**, the **calendar**, and your **notification**
settings.

You won't see every screen here — what's in your sidebar depends on your role. A few features
(posting announcements, moderating chat rooms, sending company-wide banners) are limited to
managers and admins and are marked with a 🔒 callout.

## Email

IE Central has a full email program built right in, so you can read and send mail without leaving
the app. You connect your own mailbox (your IE Tires address, or a personal Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo,
or iCloud account) and it appears in a familiar three-pane layout: folders on the left, your list
of messages in the middle, and the open message on the right.

Open **Email** from the sidebar to get started.

### Connecting your mailbox

The first time you use Email, you need to connect an account.

1. Open **Email**, then go to **Email Accounts** (or click **Add Account**).
2. Under **Add Account**, pick the type of mailbox you have:
   - **Gmail** — *Connect with Google*
   - **Outlook** — *Connect with Microsoft*
   - **Yahoo Mail** — *Connect with Yahoo*
   - **iCloud Mail** — *Use app-specific password*
   - **Custom IMAP** — *Any IMAP/SMTP server* (this is the option for your IE Tires email)
3. For Gmail, Outlook, or Yahoo, you'll be sent to that provider's sign-in page. Log in and approve
   access, and you're done.
4. For **iCloud**, follow the on-screen steps to create an *app-specific password*, then fill in
   your **iCloud Email Address**, the **App-Specific Password**, and an optional **Display Name**,
   and click **Connect iCloud Account**.
5. For **Custom IMAP** (including IE Tires mail), the page will often detect your provider and show
   **Use This Configuration** — click it to fill in the technical settings for you. Otherwise enter
   your **Email Address**, **Password**, and server details, then click **Connect Account**.

Your first connected account becomes your **Primary** mailbox. You can connect more than one and
switch between them using the account name at the top of the folder list.

> **Tip:** Settings vary by provider. If a setup page offers **Use This Configuration**, take it —
> it spares you from typing server names and port numbers.

### Reading mail

1. Pick a folder on the left — **Inbox**, **Sent**, **Drafts**, **Trash**, **Spam**, **Archive**,
   or any folder you've made.
2. Click a message in the middle list to open it on the right. Unread messages show a dot.
3. While reading, use the icons at the top of the message to **Star**, **Mark as unread**,
   **Delete**, or **Add to Calendar**.
4. To search, type into the **Search emails...** box above the message list.

If a message has files attached, you'll see an **Attachments** area. Click an attachment to preview
it. From the preview you can **Download** it, or click **Save to DocHub** to file it in the company
document hub (give it a name, pick a **Category**, and click **Save to DocHub**).

### Writing and replying

1. Click **Compose** (the pencil button) to start a new message — or open a message and click
   **Reply**, **Reply All**, or **Forward**.
2. Add recipients in the **To:** field. Start typing a name or address and pick from the
   suggestions. Click **Cc** or **Bcc** if you need them.
3. Type a **Subject** and write your message in the body. Use the formatting bar for **bold**,
   *italic*, underline, and bulleted or numbered lists.
4. To attach a file, click the paperclip in the formatting bar and choose your file.
5. Click **Send**.

> **Tip:** Drafts save automatically as you type, so you won't lose a half-written message. You can
> always find it later in the **Drafts** folder.

### Folders, signatures, and tidying up

- **Create a folder** with the small **+** next to **FOLDERS** in the sidebar.
- Set up an email **Signature** from the button at the bottom of the folder list (or under
  **Account Settings**). You get a full editor with formatting, links, and images, plus a
  **Preview** before you **Save Signature**.
- To act on several messages at once, select them and use the toolbar that appears: **Archive**,
  move to **Trash**, **Mark as read** / **Mark as unread**, **Star**, **Snooze** (for example
  *Later today*, *Tomorrow*, or *Next week*), or **Move to folder**.

## Messages & chat

There are a few different ways to message people in IE Central, depending on who you're trying to
reach.

| Way to message | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Direct & group messages** | Quick back-and-forth chat with a coworker or a small group, inside IE Central |
| **Chat rooms** | Department- or location-wide rooms in the mobile employee app (some are moderated) |
| **Broadcast banners** | Short company notices that appear as a card on the dashboard |

### Direct and group messages

Open **Messages** from the sidebar for real-time chat with coworkers.

1. Click **New Conversation**.
2. Choose **Direct Message** to chat with one person, or **Group Chat** for several.
3. Use **Search users...** to find people. For a group, also type a **Group name...**, then click
   **Create Group**.
4. Type in the **Message...** box and press send (the paper-plane button).

While chatting you can:
- Add an emoji, send a **GIF**, or **Attach file**.
- Hover a message to **Add reaction** (👍, ❤️, 😂, and more).
- Mention a coworker with **@**, or link to a project, applicant, document, or person with **#**.
- **Mute notifications** for a conversation if it gets noisy.
- Start a video call right from the conversation with the **Start Video Call** button in the
  header — this opens an IE Central meeting (see [Meetings & video](#meetings--video)).

### Chat rooms

Chat rooms are shared rooms organized by **department** or **location**, used in the IE Central
mobile employee app. Everyone in the matching department or location can read and post. They work
like a group conversation for a whole team, rather than a private message to one person.

Some rooms are **moderated** — that means a manager reviews posts before they appear for everyone.
If a room is moderated, your message may wait for approval before others see it. That's normal.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — approving, rejecting, or removing messages in a moderated room is
> done by managers.

### Broadcast banners

Now and then a colored notice card appears at the top of your **Dashboard** — for example an
*Important Update*. These are company **broadcasts**. Read it, and click the **X** in the corner to
dismiss it once you've seen it. Higher-priority notices are flagged **Important**.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — posting a company-wide banner is done with the **Broadcast** button
> on the dashboard, available to administrators.

## Announcements

Announcements are official company messages — policy updates, schedule news, reminders — that can
be sent to everyone or to a specific department or location. Employees read announcements in the
mobile employee app and can be alerted by a push notification.

### Posting an announcement

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — most employees won't see the Announcements management screen.

1. Open **Announcements** from the sidebar and click **New Announcement**.
2. Fill in a **Title** and the **Content**.
3. Set the **Priority** to **Normal** or **Urgent**.
4. Choose the **Target Audience**: **All Employees**, **Specific Departments**, or **Specific
   Locations** (pick the departments or locations if you choose one of those).
5. Optionally turn on **Pin to top**, set an **Expires At** date, and tick **Send push
   notification** to alert people right away.
6. Click **Create Announcement**.

After posting you can **Edit**, **Deactivate** / **Activate**, or **Delete** any announcement, and
each one shows how many people have read it (for example *12 reads*).

## Meetings & video

IE Central includes its own video meeting system — HD video, screen sharing, and automatic AI
meeting notes — so you can hold a call without any outside app. Open **Meetings** from the sidebar.

### Starting or scheduling a meeting

1. Click **New Meeting**.
2. Choose **Start Now** to meet immediately, or **Schedule for Later** to set a date and time.
3. Enter a **Meeting Title** (for a scheduled meeting also set the **Date**, **Start Time**, and
   **End Time**).
4. If you'd like an automatic transcript and summary, tick **Noted Meeting (AI transcription +
   notes)** (see [AI meeting notes](#ai-meeting-notes) below).
5. Click **Start Now** or **Schedule Meeting**.

### Joining a meeting

- From **Meetings**, your scheduled calls appear under **Upcoming Meetings** — click **Join** next
  to one.
- To join someone else's call, find the **Join a Meeting** area, type the code into **Enter meeting
  code**, and click **Join**.
- Past calls are listed under **Past Meetings**; ones with notes show a **Noted** badge and a
  **View Notes** button.

### In a meeting

Along the bottom of the meeting you'll find the controls:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mute** / **Unmute** | Turn your microphone off or on |
| **Turn off camera** / **Turn on camera** | Turn your video off or on |
| **Share screen** / **Stop sharing** | Show your screen to everyone |
| **Start noting** / **Stop noting** | Turn the AI notes recording on or off |
| **Participants** | See who's in the call |
| **Leave call** | Exit the meeting |

Hosts can also click **Invite** to share the **Join Code** and **Join URL**, or send an email
invite to an outside guest by entering their **Name** and **Email** and clicking **Send Email
Invite**.

### The Companion App (optional)

For full remote desktop control during a meeting — actually moving the mouse and typing on the
other person's computer, not just seeing it — there's the **IECentral Companion App**. From the
**Meetings** page you can download it for **macOS** or **Windows**. You only need it if you want to
give or take real remote control; ordinary video, audio, and screen sharing work without it.

### AI meeting notes

If a meeting was a **Noted Meeting**, IE Central automatically creates written notes after it ends —
you don't have to take any. Open the meeting under **Past Meetings** and click **View Notes**.

You'll see the meeting process through a few stages (*Transcribing*, *Generating Notes*, then
**Complete**), after which the notes include:

- A **Summary** of the conversation
- **Action Items** — with checkboxes you can tick off as they're done
- **Decisions Made**
- **Follow-ups**
- **Key Topics**
- The **Full Transcript** (in a collapsible section)

Click **Save to DocHub** to keep a copy in the company document hub.

## Zoom

If your team uses Zoom, you can connect your Zoom account so IE Central can create Zoom links for
you and pull your Zoom meetings into the calendar.

1. Open **Calendar** from the sidebar.
2. Start a new event and set its **Meeting Type** to **📹 Zoom**.
3. If you haven't connected Zoom yet, you'll see **Connect Zoom** — click it and sign in to Zoom to
   link your account. Once connected, the event shows *Connected as* your Zoom email, and a Zoom
   link is created for you automatically.
4. To pull in Zoom meetings you were invited to by email, click **Sync Zoom** on the calendar. New
   Zoom meetings show up as calendar events.

> **Tip:** If you'd rather use an existing Zoom link, you can paste it into the meeting link field
> instead of connecting your account.

## Calendar & events

The **Calendar** shows your events and meetings in month, week, or day views, and lets you create
events, invite people, and attach meeting links. Open **Calendar** from the sidebar.

### Getting around the calendar

- Use the left/right arrows to move between months, and **Today** to jump back to the current date.
- If coworkers have shared their calendars with you, switch between them with the calendar picker
  at the top (**My Calendar**, then each shared one).
- A **pending** badge appears when you have meeting invitations waiting for a reply.

### Creating an event

1. Click **+ New Event**.
2. Enter a **Title** and set the **Start** and **End** times.
3. Choose a **Meeting Type**:
   - **🎯 IECentral Meeting** — creates a built-in video room with a join code automatically
   - **📹 Zoom** — creates a Zoom link (see [Zoom](#zoom))
   - **💼 Microsoft Teams**, **🎥 Google Meet**, **📞 Phone Call**, **🏢 In Person**, or **🔗
     Other**
4. Add a **Location**, a **Description**, and use **Invite Users** to invite coworkers.
5. To make it recurring, set **Repeat** (for example *Daily*, *Weekly*, or *Monthly*).
6. Click **Create Event**.

### Replying to invitations

- Invitations you've received appear under **Pending Invitations** — click **Accept** or
  **Decline**.
- Opening an event shows a **Your Response** section where you can choose **Accept**, **Maybe**, or
  **Decline**. For built-in video events you'll also see **Join Meeting**.

### Sharing your calendar

Click **Share** to let a coworker see your calendar. If you're the organizer of an event, you can
also **Edit Event**, **+ Add Invitees**, **Cancel This Event**, or **Cancel Entire Series** for a
recurring event.

## Notifications

IE Central can alert you about new messages, meeting notes, reviews, schedule changes, and more.
Open **Notifications** from the sidebar to see them all.

### Reading and clearing notifications

1. Switch between the **All** and **Unread** tabs.
2. For any item, click **View Details** to go to what it's about, **Mark as read**, or **Dismiss**
   to clear it.
3. Click **Mark all as read** to clear everything at once.

When you're caught up you'll see *All caught up!*

### Turning on push notifications

Push notifications pop up even when IE Central isn't open in front of you, so you don't miss
important messages.

1. You may see a small **Enable Notifications** prompt — click **Enable** to turn them on, or **Not
   now** to skip it.
2. You can also toggle them any time from the bell icon on the **Notifications** page. The tooltip
   tells you whether push is currently *enabled* or whether you can *Enable push notifications*.
3. Your browser will ask for permission the first time — choose **Allow**.

> **Tip:** If you said "Not now" and want them later, just use the bell icon on the
> **Notifications** page to turn them on.
