finding screens, searching, keeping your profile and password up to date, and turning
on notifications. No technical background needed — everything here is something you can

## Logging in <a id="logging-in"></a>

IE Central uses your work **email address** and a **password**. Your account is set up
for you by a manager or administrator — you don't sign up on your own.

1. Open IE Central in your web browser (or the installed app).
2. On the **Sign in** screen, type your work email in the **Email** box (for example,
   `you@ietires.com`).
3. Type your **Password**.
4. Click **Sign in**.

When the login succeeds, you land on your home screen — for most people the **Dashboard**,
and for warehouse and retail employees their **Portal** home page.

**Tips**

- If you see the message **"Invalid email or password,"** the email, the password, or
  both don't match. For your security, the message never tells you which one was wrong —
  re-check both and try again.
- The first time you log in (or after an administrator resets your account), you'll be
  taken straight to the **Change Password** screen and asked to set your own password
  before you can continue. See [Changing your password](#changing-your-password).
- The **Join a Meeting** button under the sign-in box is only for entering a meeting code
  as a guest — you don't need to log in to use it.

## If you're locked out or forgot your password <a id="locked-out"></a>

There is **no "Forgot password?" link** on the sign-in screen. Password resets are done
by a manager or administrator.

1. Contact your manager or the IE Central administrator and ask them to **reset your
   password**.
2. They'll give you a temporary password.
3. Sign in with that temporary password. IE Central will immediately send you to the
   **Change Password** screen to pick your own.

If your account has been turned off (deactivated), you won't be able to sign in at all,
even with the right password — ask your manager to reactivate it.

## Changing your password <a id="changing-your-password"></a>

You can change your password any time, and you'll be required to change it the first time
you log in.

1. If you're being *required* to change it, the **Change Password** screen appears on its
   own after you sign in, with the note "You must change your password before continuing."
2. To change it on your own later, open **Settings** from the sidebar and go to the
   **Security** tab. (If you don't see **Settings** in your sidebar, ask an administrator —
   see [Roles & permissions](#roles).)
3. Enter your **Current Password**.
4. Enter your **New Password**, then re-type it in **Confirm New Password**.
5. Click **Update Password**.

**Rules and tips**

- Your new password must be **at least 8 characters**.
- The two new-password boxes must match exactly, or you'll see "New passwords do not match."
- Pick something you'll remember but others can't guess — there's no automatic reset link,
  so a forgotten password means contacting an administrator.

## Finding your way around <a id="navigation"></a>

Most people navigate IE Central from the **sidebar** on the left.

**The sidebar**

- At the top is the **IE Central** logo (with a small **BETA** tag).
- Below it are the main shortcuts — typically **Dashboard**, **Messages**, **Email**,
  **Calendar**, **Meetings**, and **Notifications**.
- Under those are collapsible groups such as **People & HR**, **Equipment**, **Finance**,
  **Tools**, and **System**. Click a group's name to expand it and reveal the screens
  inside; click again to collapse it.
- Some menu items show a small **red or blue number badge** — that's a count of unread
  items (for example, unread messages, emails, or notifications).
- At the very bottom is **your name and email**, with a **Sign out** button (the
  door-and-arrow icon) next to it.

**On a phone or small screen**

- The sidebar is hidden to save space. Tap the **menu button** (three stacked lines) in
  the top-left to slide it open, and tap anywhere outside it to close it.
- The mobile top bar also has a **notification bell** and a **search** icon.

**The home dashboard**

After logging in, office and management users see the **Dashboard** — a set of cards
summarizing your day: **Day at a Glance** (today's calendar events), recent **Projects**
and **Applications**, **Website Messages**, an **Activity Feed**, **Email**, and more.
Warehouse and retail employees instead land on their **Portal** home page (schedule, hours,
time off, paystubs).

- The cards you see depend on your role — you won't have cards for things you can't access.
- On the Dashboard you can click the **gear icon** (top-right, "Customize Dashboard") to
  turn cards on or off and drag them into the order you like.
- The **?** (question mark) icon opens a help panel explaining that screen.

## Why the app looks different for different people <a id="appearance"></a>

Two things make IE Central look different from one person to the next:

1. **Your role.** Your menus are tailored to what your job needs. A warehouse associate, an
   office manager, and a system administrator each see a different set of items in the
   sidebar. If a screen described in this guide isn't in your menu, you most likely don't
   have access to it — that's normal.
2. **Your chosen look ("Layout Mode").** IE Central can be displayed in several styles:
   - **Modern** — the standard look, with the sidebar menu on the left. This is what most
     people use and what this guide describes.
   - **Desktop** — a "computer desktop" style with clickable icons, a taskbar, and movable
     windows you can open, resize, and minimize.
   - **JMK Terminal** — a retro, text-menu style (a tribute to the old system), where you
     pick numbered menu items.
   - A few other fun visual themes (**Pip-Boy**, **Amber CRT**, **Dracula**) that just
     change the colors while keeping the normal sidebar.

The wording and exact menu placement in this guide assume the **Modern** look. If yours
looks different, the same features are still there — just in a different style. See
[Settings & appearance options](#settings) for how to switch (where available).

## Global search — jump to anything fast <a id="global-search"></a>

Global search is the fastest way to get to a specific person, document, piece of equipment,
tire, project, or other record without hunting through menus.

**Open it**

- Press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows), **or**
- Click the **Search...** box in the top bar (on a phone, tap the magnifying-glass icon).

**Use it**

1. Start typing what you're looking for. You need **at least 2 characters** before it
   searches — until then it shows "Type at least 2 characters to search."
2. Results appear instantly and are **grouped by category** — for example **People**,
   **Documents**, **Tires**, and **Operations** (equipment, projects, locations, and more).
3. Keep typing to **narrow the results**.
4. Move through results with the **up and down arrow keys**, then press **Enter** to open
   the highlighted one. You can also just click any result with your mouse.
5. Press **Esc** (or click outside the box) to close search.

**What clicking a result does**

Each result takes you straight to the right screen:

- A **document** opens in **Doc Hub** with its preview.
- A **tire** opens the inventory report focused on that item.
- A **person** opens their personnel page; an **application** opens that applicant.
- **Equipment**, **projects**, **announcements**, and **locations** each open their screen.

**Good to know**

- Search only shows you things you're **allowed to see**. Two people searching the same
  word may get different results based on their roles.
- If you see "Showing top matches — refine your search," there are more results than fit;
  type a few more characters to narrow them down.

## Your profile <a id="your-profile"></a>

To see your account details:

1. Open **Settings** from the sidebar.
2. On the **Profile** tab you'll see your **Name**, **Email**, and **Role**.

These fields are **read-only** here — they're managed by HR and administrators. If your
name, email, or role is wrong, ask your manager or an administrator to update it. (Your
name and email also appear at the bottom of the sidebar at all times.)

> If you don't see **Settings** in your sidebar, your role doesn't include it. Your name
> and email are still visible at the bottom of the sidebar, and password changes can be
> done by an administrator on your behalf.

## Notifications <a id="notifications"></a>

IE Central can alert you about new messages, schedule changes, and other important updates.

**Where they appear**

- The **notification bell** — in the sidebar (the **Notifications** item) on a computer,
  and in the top bar on a phone. A **red number badge** shows how many you haven't read.
- Open **Notifications** to see the full list. Use the **All** and **Unread** tabs to
  filter, and click **Mark all as read** to clear the unread count.

**Turn on push notifications (alerts on your device)**

Push notifications let IE Central alert you even when you don't have it open in front of you.

1. A small **"Enable Notifications"** pop-up may appear in the corner shortly after you log
   in. Click **Enable** to turn them on, or **Not now** to dismiss it (it'll ask again
   later).
2. You can also turn them on any time from the **Notifications** screen: click the **bell
   icon** in the top-right of that screen. When it's highlighted, push is on ("Push
   notifications enabled"); click it again to turn push off.
3. The first time, your browser or phone will ask for permission to show notifications —
   choose **Allow**.

**Tips**

- If you previously chose **Block** or **Deny** in your browser, IE Central can't turn
  notifications back on for you. You'll need to re-allow them in your browser's or phone's
  site settings first.
- Push notifications aren't available in every browser. If you don't see the bell toggle,
  your current browser doesn't support them.

## Settings & appearance options <a id="settings"></a>

Open **Settings** from the sidebar (under the **System** group). It's organized into tabs:

| Tab | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | View your name, email, and role. Choose your **theme** and **Layout Mode**. |
| **Security** | Change your password. |
| **Users** | Add and manage team members. 🔒 Admins only. |
| **Locations** | Set warehouse-manager contact info per location. 🔒 Admins only. |

On the **Profile** tab you can change two looks:

- **Appearance (theme):** **Light** (the clean, default look) or **Dark**.
- **Layout Mode:** **Modern**, **Desktop**, **JMK Terminal**, **Pip-Boy**, **Amber CRT**,
  or **Dracula** — see [why the app looks different](#appearance). Click the style you want;
  it switches right away and is remembered on that device.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — the full **Settings** screen (including the **Users** and
> **Locations** tabs) is limited to administrators. Most employees won't see **Settings** in
> their sidebar at all. If you need a password reset or a profile correction and can't get to
> Settings, ask your manager or an administrator.

## Roles & permissions — why some people see more <a id="roles"></a>

IE Central gives each person a **role** (you can see yours on the **Profile** tab). Your
role decides which menus and screens appear for you. That's why two coworkers can have very
different-looking sidebars.

- A **warehouse or retail employee** sees a focused **Portal**: schedule, hours, time off,
  paystubs, surveys, and announcements.
- A **department manager** sees their department tools plus messages and notifications.
- **Office managers and higher** see the full Dashboard and business modules.
- **Administrators** see everything, including system tools.

A few screens are reserved for managers and administrators:

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — **Users** (managing accounts), the **Audit Log** (a record
> of who did what), and **Deleted Records** (restoring removed items) are administrator-only.
> Most employees won't see these in their sidebar.

If you believe you should have access to something that isn't showing up, ask your manager
or an administrator — they can adjust your role or grant you the specific access you need.
