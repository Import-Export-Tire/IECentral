# 6 — Operations & Field

This section covers the day-to-day operations tools: storing and finding documents in **Doc Hub**,
completing **safety checklists** before you operate equipment, filing a **safety report**, raising an
anonymous concern through **See Something, Say Something**, keeping your **daily log**, tracking
work on the **Projects** board, and viewing **equipment**, **locations**, the **org chart**, and the
employee and department **portals**.

You won't have access to every screen here — what you see depends on your role. Features limited to
managers, admins, or super-admins are marked with a 🔒 callout.

## Doc Hub — documents & folders

**What it's for:** Doc Hub is your shared filing cabinet. It holds forms, policies, SOPs, training
materials, templates, and any other company documents — organized into folders you can browse,
search, preview, and share. Open **Documents** from the sidebar to get there.

Everything you upload is **private by default** — only you can see it until you choose to share it.

### Finding a document

1. Open **Documents** from the sidebar.
2. Browse the folders on the left, or click into a folder to see what's inside. The trail of folder
   names across the top (the "breadcrumbs") shows where you are — click any part of it to jump back
   up, or click **Doc Hub** to return to the top.
3. To search, type in the **Search...** box. It looks across document names, descriptions, and
   filenames you have access to, and shows both matching **Files** and matching **Folders**.
4. Use the **Grid view** / **List view** buttons in the toolbar to switch between picture-style cards
   and a compact table. Use the category pills (**All**, **Forms**, **Policies**, **SOPs**,
   **Templates**, **Training**, **Other**) to filter what's shown.

### Folder cards: file & subfolder counts

Each folder card shows how much is inside it, for example **"2 folders · 14 files"**. The file count
includes everything in that folder and all of its subfolders. A folder with nothing in it shows
**"Empty"**.

### Opening and previewing a document

1. Click a document to open the preview window.
2. **Word and Excel files open as a preview** — the first time you open an Office file it shows
   **"Converting to PDF…"** for a few seconds while it prepares a readable version. After that it
   opens quickly.
3. From the preview window you can use **Open in new tab**, **Print**, or **Download**.
4. If a preview ever fails to load, use the **Open in new tab** or **Download** buttons to get the
   file directly.

> **Tip:** You can also right-click any file and choose **Preview**, **Download**, **Share**, or
> **Version History** (to see or restore older versions).

### Uploading a file

1. Click the **Upload** button (or just drag a file onto the page — you'll see **"Drop files to
   upload"**).
2. In the **Upload Document** window, drop your file or click **"Click to browse or drag a file
   here"**.
3. Give it a **Document Name**, an optional **Description**, and pick a **Category** (Forms, Policies,
   SOPs, Templates, Training, or Other).
4. Under **Who can see this?**, choose **Just Me**, **Team**, or **Everyone**.
5. (Optional) Open **Advanced options** to set an **Expiration Date** or require an e-signature.
6. Click **Upload Document**.

### Uploading a whole folder

To bring in a folder along with all its subfolders at once:

1. Click **Upload Folder**.
2. Pick the folder on your computer. Its subfolders and files are recreated in Doc Hub exactly as
   they are arranged. Hidden system files (like `.DS_Store`) are skipped.
3. Watch the progress count, then click **Close** when it's done.

> **Tip:** Folder upload works best in **Chrome**. If your browser opens it in single-file mode by
> mistake, hard-refresh the page and try again.

### Organizing into folders

1. Click **New Folder** (the folder-plus button near the top).
2. Enter a **Folder Name** and optional **Description**, then choose a **Privacy Level**.
3. Click **Create Folder**.
4. To move things around, drag a file onto a folder to move it in, or drag a folder onto another
   folder to nest it inside.

### Password-protected folders

Some folders hold confidential or HIPAA-style information and are **password-protected**. When you
open one, you'll be asked for the **folder password**. Type it in to view the contents.

- Access to protected folders is **logged** for compliance.
- For privacy, **even admins cannot bypass a folder password** — if you don't have it, ask the folder
  owner.

> **Tip:** Right-click a folder and choose **Manage Access** to share it with specific people or
> with a group (for example "Warehouse" or "Management") so they don't need a password.

## Safety checklists

**What it's for:** Before you operate certain equipment (such as a picker), you must complete a quick
**Pre-Operation Safety Check**. You usually start this by scanning the QR code on the equipment, which
opens the checklist on your phone or scanner.

### Completing a safety check

1. Open the checklist (usually by scanning the equipment's QR code). You'll see the
   **Pre-Operation Safety Check** screen.
2. Under **Select Your Name**, choose your name from the list, then tap **Start Safety Check**.
   - Only people who have completed the required training (e.g. the **Picker Training Video**) appear
     in the list.
   - **Important:** Selecting someone else's name is a terminable offense. If your name isn't listed,
     see your manager before going further.
3. Work through each item one at a time. The screen shows **Step X of Y** and your percent complete.
   For each item, answer **YES**, **NO**, or (when offered) **N/A**.
4. Some items make you **wait a few seconds** before you can continue — you'll see **"Wait Xs"**
   change to **"Ready to proceed"** when you can move on. This makes sure each item is actually
   checked.
5. If an item needs an explanation (for example, damage you noticed), add it in the **Notes** field.
   Some answers require details before you can continue.
6. When you finish you'll see one of two results:
   - **All Clear!** — the equipment passed; you may operate it.
   - **Issues Found** — one or more items failed. **Report to your supervisor before operating.**

> **Tip:** To run another check, tap **Start New Check**.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — The **Safety Check Manager** screen lets managers review who completed
> checks by **Date** and **Location**, see totals (**Total Checks**, **All Passed**, **With Issues**),
> and print records. Use **Print Record** for a single inspection or **Print Daily Report** for the
> whole day's compliance report.

## Safety reports

**What it's for:** Safety reports are the formal incident records reviewed and printed by safety
leadership. (If you simply want to *raise* a concern, use
[See Something, Say Something](#see-something-say-something) below — it's anonymous and open to
everyone.)

> 🔒 **Super-Admin only** — The **See Something, Say Something** inbox is where reports land for
> review.

### Reviewing reports

1. Open the safety reports inbox. Reports are listed with a reference code like **SR-12345**.
2. Filter by status using **All**, **New**, **In review**, **Resolved**, or **Dismissed**.
3. Click a report to open it. You'll see its **Category**, **Location**, **When**, who **Submitted**
   it (or **Anonymous**), the **Description**, and any **Photo**.
4. Set the **Status** and add internal **Review notes**, then click **Save**.

### Printing the one-page report

1. Open the report you want to print.
2. Click **Print**. This produces a clean, single-page **See Something, Say Something — Report** with
   the reference code, category, location, description, photo, and your review notes. It's marked
   **Confidential** for handling per company policy.

## See Something, Say Something

**What it's for:** This is the **anonymous** way to report a safety hazard, suspicious activity,
theft, harassment, or any other concern — or even share an idea to make the workplace better. You do
**not** have to sign in or give your name.

You'll usually reach it by **scanning a posted QR code** (on a poster around the building) or by
opening the report link directly. The form takes about two minutes.

### Submitting a concern

1. Scan the QR code on a poster, or open the report form. You'll see the
   **See Something, Say Something** page, which reminds you the form is **anonymous**.
2. Under **What would you like to report?**, pick a category:
   - **Safety hazard** — an unsafe condition, equipment, or practice
   - **Security / suspicious activity**
   - **Theft** — missing inventory, cash, or property
   - **Harassment / misconduct**
   - **Something else**
3. In **What did you see?**, describe what happened, with as much detail as you're comfortable
   sharing.
4. (Optional) In **When did this happen?**, note roughly when (for example "this morning" or
   "ongoing").
5. (Optional) Tap **Take or choose a photo** to attach a picture.
6. (Optional) Only if you *want* someone to follow up, tap **"+ Add my contact info"** and fill in a
   name, phone, or email. Leave it blank to stay completely anonymous.
7. Tap **Submit report**.
8. You'll see a **Thank you** confirmation with a **Reference number**. Note it down if you'd like to
   refer to your report later. You can then close the page.

### Your anonymity is protected

- **No login, no tracking.** The form doesn't record who you are, your login, or your network address
  — just your report.
- **Photos are scrubbed.** Any photo you attach has its **location and device data automatically
  removed**, so a picture can't be traced back to you.
- Reports are taken seriously and investigated. IE Tires does not tolerate retaliation against anyone
  who speaks up in good faith. (You're asked to be truthful and avoid untrue or misrepresented facts.)

> 🔒 **Super-Admin only** — Reports go to a small, restricted group of **super-admins** for review.
> No one else can see them. Super-admins manage the printable per-location QR posters from the
> **Print QR posters** link in the inbox.

## Daily log & tasks

**What it's for:** The daily log is where you record what you worked on each day. Some roles are asked
to submit one daily. It also keeps your recurring **daily tasks** in one place.

### Logging your day

1. Open your daily log from the sidebar.
2. Fill in:
   - **Summary** — a short recap of your day
   - **Accomplishments** — what you got done (you can click **+ Add another** for more lines)
   - **Hours Worked**
   - **Blockers** (optional) — anything that held you up
   - **Goals for Tomorrow** (optional)
3. While you're still working on it, it shows **Draft in Progress**. When you're ready, submit it —
   it then shows **Log Submitted** and is locked.

> **Tip:** The system also notes some of your activity automatically (such as projects you moved or
> tasks you completed) and adds it to your log.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — Managers see the **Team Daily Logs** view with everyone's logs,
> **Today's Live Activity**, day streaks, and totals. They can add **Reviewer Notes** (not visible to
> the submitter), submit a log on someone's behalf with **Submit on Behalf**, and **Print** or
> **Export** reports.

## Projects & jobs

**What it's for:** The **Projects** board is a Kanban board for tracking work. Each project is a card
that moves across columns as it progresses. Open **Projects** from the sidebar.

### The board

Projects move through four columns:

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Backlog** | Not started yet |
| **In Progress** | Being worked on |
| **Review** | Done, awaiting a check |
| **Done** | Finished |

### Creating a project card

1. Click **New Project**.
2. In **Create New Project**, enter a **Project Name** and **Description**, set a **Priority** (Low,
   Medium, High, Urgent), and optionally an estimate, **Due Date**, and who to **Assign To**.
3. Click **Create Project**. The card appears in **Backlog**.

### Moving a card

- **Drag and drop** a card from one column to the next to update its status (the page reminds you:
  "Drag and drop to update status"). An empty column shows **"Drop projects here"**.

### Inside a project

Click a card to open it. From there you can:

- Add tasks with **Add Task**, or click **AI Generate** to have the system suggest a task list from
  the project description.
- See **Tasks Assigned to You** and mark them **todo**, **in progress**, or **done**.
- Add **Progress Notes** — type **@** to mention a teammate and notify them.
- Use **Share Project** to share it, **Edit Project** to change details, or **Delete Project** to
  remove it.

### Jobs

The **Jobs** screen (**Job Listings**) is for managing recruiting postings shown on the IE Tire
careers page — not day-to-day project work. See
[Section 3 — Hiring & Growth](03-hiring-and-growth.md) for hiring and applications.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — Posting and editing jobs is restricted.

## Equipment & vehicles

**What it's for:** The **Equipment** screen tracks the company's physical gear — order **Pickers**,
company **Vehicles**, and **Computers** — including who has each item and its condition. Open
**Equipment** from the sidebar. (Warehouse **Scanners** have their own **Scanner Fleet** screen — see
[Scanner setup](#scanner-setup).)

### Viewing equipment

1. Open **Equipment**.
2. Switch between the **Pickers**, **Vehicles**, and **Computers** tabs.
3. Each item shows its status, such as **Available**, **Inactive**, or **Inoperable**.
4. For a picker, you can open its **QR** code, view its **History**, or view its **Safety History**.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — Assigning and returning equipment is restricted.
>
> - **Assign** hands an item to a person and captures a signed responsibility agreement.
> - **Return** runs a condition checklist (Physical Condition, Screen Functional, Buttons Working,
>   Battery Condition, Charging Port OK, Scanner Functional, Clean Condition) and an **Overall
>   Condition** of good, fair, or poor.
> - **Reassign** moves an item from one person to another in one step.
> - **Retire** and **Delete** take an item out of service (Delete is admin-only).
>
> Under **Remote Access Computers**, **Connect via Chrome Remote** opens a remote session to a
> managed computer.

### Vehicles

The **Vehicles** tab tracks company vehicles, including registration and insurance dates and mileage.
Use **Add Vehicle** to add one and **Assign** to put it with a driver.

## Locations & org chart

### Locations

**What it's for:** The **Locations** screen lists every company location — warehouses, retail, office,
and distribution sites. Open **Locations** from the sidebar to see **Active Locations** and
**Inactive Locations**.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — Adding (**Add Location**), editing, and deactivating locations is
> restricted. Sensitive details such as door PINs, alarm codes, gate codes, and Wi-Fi passwords are
> only shown to admins.

### Org chart

**What it's for:** The **Organization Chart** shows the company's structure grouped by role level.
Open **Org Chart** from the sidebar.

People are grouped by tier:

| Tier | Label |
|---|---|
| T5 | **Super Admin** |
| T4 | **Admin** |
| T3 | **Director** |
| T2 | **Manager** |
| T1 | **Shift Lead** |

Special responsibilities are flagged with badges such as **Final Approver**, **Payroll**, and
**Daily Log**.

## Portals

IE Central has two "portal" dashboards that pull your most common tasks into one place.

### Employee Portal

**What it's for:** The **Employee Portal** is your personal self-service home. It shows your current
clock **Status** and **Hours Today**, lets you clock in and out, and links to your everyday tasks.

From the portal you can:

- **Clock In** / **Clock Out** and **Start Break** / **End Break**.
- See **Today's Schedule**.
- Open the quick-link tiles: **Schedule**, **My Hours**, **Time Off**, **Call Off**, **Paystubs**,
  **Corrections**, and **Documents**.

> Most of these tools are covered in detail in [Section 2 — Time & Pay](02-time-and-pay.md).

### Department Portal

**What it's for:** The **Department Portal** is for department leads to see their team for the day. It
shows each department you manage, who's on the crew (**Your Crew Today**), the day's goals
(**Today's Goals**), and each person's clock status. Use the date controls (including **Today**) to
look at a specific day.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — The Department Portal is shown to department leads and managers.

## Scanner setup

**What it's for:** Warehouse barcode scanners (Zebra TC51 handhelds) are set up and managed on the
**Scanner Fleet** screen. Most employees just *use* a scanner that's already set up — this screen is
for the people who provision and look after the fleet.

> 🔒 **Admin only** — Setting up and managing scanners is restricted.
>
> From **Equipment**, click **Scanner Fleet** to see all devices, their **Online**/**Offline**
> status, battery level, location, and who they're assigned to. To set up a new device, click
> **Setup New Scanner** — a step-by-step wizard (**Detect → Location → Identity → Generate →
> Install → Verify → Done**) walks you through connecting the scanner and installing the apps. The
> setup wizard runs in the **Chrome** browser.
