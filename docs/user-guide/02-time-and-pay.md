# 2 — Time & Pay

This is the part of IE Central you'll use the most: clocking in and out, checking your hours,
requesting time off, viewing your pay stubs, and submitting mileage or expenses. Managers and
admins also approve everyone's time here.

Most hourly employees land on the **Employee Portal** the moment they log in — a simple, phone-
friendly screen with a big clock and a row of buttons. Managers and admins instead see the full
sidebar with the management screens described later in this section.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** sections are marked throughout. Most employees won't see those
> screens — that's normal.

## The Employee Portal {#the-employee-portal}

When you log in as an hourly employee, IE Central takes you straight to your **Employee Portal**.
It's built to be quick on a phone or a shared screen.

At the top you'll see:

- **Welcome back** with your name, a large live clock, and today's date.
- A **Status** card showing whether you're **Clocked In**, **On Break**, **Clocked Out**, or
  **Not Clocked In**, plus your **Hours Today**.
- The clock buttons (**Clock In**, **Start Break**, **Clock Out**) — see below.
- **Today's Schedule**, if you're assigned a shift today (your department and shift times; a
  **Department Lead** badge appears if you're the lead).

Below that is a grid of buttons that jump to the rest of your tools: **Schedule**, **My Hours**,
**Time Off**, **Call Off**, **Paystubs**, **Corrections**, and **Documents**.

**Tip:** To leave the portal, use the **log-out** icon in the top-right corner of the header.

## Time clock — clocking in and out {#time-clock}

Your time clock lives right on the Employee Portal home screen. The buttons change depending on
your current status, so you'll only ever see the action that makes sense next.

### Clock in for the day

1. Open your **Employee Portal** (it opens automatically when you log in).
2. Check the **Status** card — it should say **Not Clocked In**.
3. Press the green **Clock In** button.
4. The status flips to **Clocked In** and **Hours Today** starts counting.

### Take a break or meal

1. While you're **Clocked In**, press **Start Break**.
2. Your status changes to **On Break**.
3. When you come back, press **End Break** to return to **Clocked In**.

> **Important:** Always press **End Break** when you return. If a break is never ended, the system
> can't subtract that break time correctly, which can throw off your daily hours.

### Clock out for the day

1. While you're **Clocked In**, press the red **Clock Out** button.
2. Your status becomes **Clocked Out** and you'll see "You're done for today!"

### About the 5-minute grace window

IE Central compares your **Clock In** time to your scheduled start time:

- You **cannot clock in early** — clocking in before your scheduled start is blocked.
- If you clock in **within 5 minutes** of your start time, you're counted as on time (this is the
  "grace period").
- If you clock in **more than 5 minutes** late, the punch is marked **late**, and your manager may
  get an alert.
- Clocking in late repeatedly (more than once in a 7-day stretch) gets flagged for your manager.

> **Tip:** Aim to clock in at or just after your scheduled start. A minute or two over is fine; the
> grace window has you covered.

### Other ways to clock in

Depending on your location, you may clock in on a **shared kiosk/tablet** at the warehouse or on a
company **mobile app** rather than your own portal. The buttons and rules are the same. If your
manager fixes a punch for you, the entry is marked as edited so there's always a clear record.

## My Hours — viewing the hours you've worked {#my-hours}

To see the hours you've already worked:

1. From the **Employee Portal**, tap **My Hours**.
2. The page shows the current **Pay Period** with your **Total Hours** and **Days Worked**.
3. A **Daily Breakdown** lists each day and the hours recorded.

If a day looks wrong or is missing, use **Time Corrections** (below) to ask your manager to fix it.

> **How hours are counted:** Each day is rounded to the nearest quarter-hour, breaks are subtracted,
> and the days are added up for the pay period. You don't need to do any of this math yourself —
> it's all shown for you.

## Time corrections — fixing a missed or wrong punch {#time-corrections}

Forgot to clock in? Clocked out at the wrong time? You can ask your manager to fix it. You can't
edit your own punches directly — you submit a request and a manager approves it.

1. From the **Employee Portal**, tap **Corrections** (titled **Time Corrections**).
2. Press **Request**.
3. Choose a **Correction Type**:
   - **Add Missed Punch** — you forgot to clock in/out/break entirely.
   - **Edit Existing Entry** — a punch is recorded but at the wrong time.
4. Pick the **Date** and the **Entry Type** (**Clock In**, **Clock Out**, **Break Start**, or
   **Break End**).
5. Enter the **Requested Time** (the correct time).
6. Type a **Reason** explaining why the fix is needed.
7. Press **Submit**.

Your request appears under **Pending Requests** until a manager reviews it. Once decided, it moves
to **Request History** marked **approved** or **denied**, and any note from your manager shows below
it.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — Managers review correction requests on the **Time Clock** screen
> (sidebar **Time Clock** → **Corrections** tab). Press **Review** on a request, then **Approve** or
> **Deny**. Approving automatically applies the fix to the employee's time.

## Timesheets & pay-period approval {#timesheets}

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — This screen is part of **Payroll** and most employees won't see it.

Pay runs on **two-week (14-day) pay periods**. Each employee's recorded time rolls up into a
timesheet for the period, which a payroll admin reviews and then locks before it's sent to
accounting.

### Regular vs. overtime

- **Regular Hours** are the first 40 hours.
- **Overtime Hours** are anything over 40, paid at the overtime rate.

These totals are calculated for you and shown per employee on the Payroll screen.

### What "approved" and "locked" mean

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **In Progress** | The pay period is current or in the future; time is still being recorded. |
| **Pending** | The period has ended but hasn't been reviewed yet. |
| **Approved** | A manager reviewed the hours and signed off. |
| **Locked** | The period is finalized for payroll. Time entries can no longer be edited. |

### Approving a pay period (admin)

1. Open **Payroll** from the sidebar.
2. Choose the company (or **All Companies**) and the pay period.
3. Review each employee's daily breakdown. Watch for **Issues** flags such as a missing clock-out
   or a pending correction.
4. Press **Approve** to sign off the period's hours.
5. When you're ready to finalize, press **Lock for Payroll**. After locking, nobody can change the
   underlying punches.
6. If you need to reopen a locked period to make a fix, press **Unlock** (admins only), correct the
   time, then approve and lock again.
7. To send the finalized hours to QuickBooks, use **Export to QuickBooks**.

> **Tip:** Always resolve flagged **Issues** (especially missing clock-outs) *before* you lock — a
> day with no clock-out won't count those hours.

## Time off & PTO {#time-off}

Request vacation, sick, and other time off, and track where each request stands.

### Request time off

1. From the **Employee Portal**, tap **Time Off**.
2. Press **Request**.
3. Choose a **Type**:

   | Type | Use it for |
   |---|---|
   | **Vacation** | Planned paid days off |
   | **Sick** | Illness or medical |
   | **Personal** | Personal days |
   | **Bereavement** | A death in the family |
   | **Other** | Anything that doesn't fit above |

4. Pick a **Start Date** and **End Date**.
5. Optionally add a **Reason**.
6. Press **Submit**.

The request shows under **Pending Requests** until a manager approves or denies it. Decided
requests move to **Request History** with an **approved** or **denied** badge and any note your
manager left.

> **Note:** Time off counts **calendar days**, including weekends. A Friday-through-Monday request
> counts as 4 days.

### See your balance

Your time-off balances (vacation/sick/personal) are tracked behind the scenes and adjust as
requests are approved. If your balance ever looks off, check with your manager — balances are set up
per position and based on your tenure.

### Cancel a request

1. Open **Time Off** and find the request under **Pending Requests**.
2. Press **Cancel Request** and confirm.

You can only cancel a request that's still **pending** (not yet approved or denied).

### Approve or deny requests (manager)

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only**

1. Open **Time Off** from the sidebar. You'll see tabs like **Pending Requests**, **Approved**, and
   **Denied**.
2. Press **Review** on a request to see the employee, dates, type, and reason.
3. Optionally add a **Manager Note** for the employee.
4. Press **Approve** or **Deny**.

If two managers open the same request, only the first decision is saved — the second sees a "already
processed" message, so requests can't be double-handled.

## Overtime offers {#overtime}

When extra hours (often a Saturday shift) are available, a manager sends out an **overtime offer** to
eligible employees. Slots are limited and filled first-come, first-served.

### Receiving and responding to an offer (employee)

When an offer is sent to you, it appears as a notification in the company **mobile app**, showing the
date, the shift times, and how many slots are left. From there you can **Accept** or **Decline**.

- While the offer is still **open**, you can change your mind and switch between accept and decline.
- Once all the **slots** are filled, accepting is closed — if you try to take the last slot after
  it's gone, you'll see that the slot is already full.

> **Tip:** Overtime is paid at 1.5× for hours over 40 in a week. Respond promptly — popular offers
> fill up fast.

### Creating an offer (manager)

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only**

1. Open **Overtime** from the sidebar.
2. Press **Create Overtime**.
3. Set the date and **Time** (defaults to 6:00 AM – 2:30 PM).
4. Choose a **Target**: **All Employees**, a **Specific Department**, a **Specific Location**, or
   specific people.
5. Set **Max Slots** (how many people can accept).
6. Save the offer. Employees are notified and can respond.

From an offer's detail view you can see who **Accepted**, **Declined**, or is still **Pending**, and
use **Send Reminders**, **Reopen**, **Delete**, or close/cancel the offer.

## Pay stubs {#pay-stubs}

1. From the **Employee Portal**, tap **Paystubs**.
2. You'll see a list of your pay stubs, each showing the **Pay Date** and the pay **Period**.
3. Tap one to see the details: **Hours**, **Gross** pay, and **Net** pay (and deductions where
   available).

If you don't see any yet, the page reads **No Paystubs Available** — stubs appear after payroll has
been processed for a period.

## Mileage {#mileage}

If you drive for work, log your trips here to be reimbursed at the current IRS mileage rate.

### Submit mileage

1. Open **Mileage** from the sidebar.
2. Press **Add Mileage**.
3. Fill in the trip:
   - **Date**
   - **From** and **To** locations
   - **Miles**
   - **Round Trip** — turn this on to count the miles both ways
   - **Purpose** of the trip
4. The **Reimbursement** amount is calculated automatically from your miles and the IRS rate.
5. **Submit** the entry.

### Mileage statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Entered but not yet submitted for approval |
| **Submitted** | Sent to your manager for approval |
| **Approved** | Approved for reimbursement |
| **Paid** | Reimbursement has been paid |

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — Admins review submitted mileage on the **Mileage** screen and use
> **Approve** and **Mark as Paid** to move entries through the workflow. You can also print a
> **Mileage Reimbursement Request** with signature lines.

## Expense reports {#expense-reports}

For out-of-pocket costs (supplies, travel, etc.), submit an itemized expense report.

### Submit an expense report

1. Open **Expense Report** from the sidebar.
2. Press **New Report**.
3. Press **Add Item** for each expense and enter the **Date**, **Description**, **Category**, and
   **Amount**, and note whether you have a **Receipt**. The **Total** adds up automatically.
4. **Save Draft** to finish later, or **Submit** to send it for approval.

You can only edit or delete a report while it's still a **draft**.

### Expense statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Still being edited; not yet submitted |
| **Submitted** | Sent for manager approval |
| **Approved** | Approved for payment |
| **Rejected** | Sent back; can be reverted to draft and resubmitted |
| **Paid** | Reimbursement has been paid |

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — Admins use **Approve**, **Reject**, and **Mark Paid** on the
> **Expense Report** screen. A rejected report can be sent back to **draft** for the employee to fix
> and resubmit. There's also a printable expense report with certification and signature lines.

## Schedule & shifts {#schedule-and-shifts}

### View your schedule (employee)

1. From the **Employee Portal**, tap **Schedule** (titled **My Schedule**).
2. You'll see your upcoming days. **Today** is highlighted, and each day shows your shift or
   **No shift scheduled**.

Your current day's shift also appears on the portal home screen under **Today's Schedule**.

### Manage the daily shift board (manager)

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only**

The **Shifts** screen is a drag-and-drop daily board for planning who works where:

- Each department has a column; drag people in to assign them, and drop someone into the lead spot
  to mark them **Lead**.
- **Copy Yesterday** carries over the previous day's layout.
- **Save as Template** / **Load Template** save and reapply a full-day plan.
- Add **Daily Goals** / **Daily Tasks** for each department.
- **Print** produces a clean copy of the day's board.

A person can't be double-booked at overlapping times, and applying a template skips anyone who's no
longer active.

### Work Schedules (shift time templates) (manager)

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only**

The **Work Schedules** screen (sidebar **Work Schedules**) is where you define reusable shift times
(for example 6:00 AM – 2:30 PM). Press **New Schedule**, name it, set the **Start Time** and **End
Time**, and **Create** it. These schedules get assigned to employees and are what the time clock
uses to decide whether someone is on time or late.

## Call-offs — reporting you can't come in {#call-offs}

If you can't make it to work, report it as soon as possible so your shift can be covered.

1. From the **Employee Portal**, tap **Call Off**.
2. Press the red **Call Off** button.
3. Confirm the **Date** (defaults to today).
4. Enter a **Reason** (required).
5. Press **Submit Call Off**. You'll see "Call off submitted successfully!"

> **Important:** Please call off as early as possible to allow time to find coverage.

Your manager is notified right away. Your recent call-offs are listed under **Recent Call Offs**,
each marked **Pending** until a manager marks it **Acknowledged**.

> 🔒 **Manager / Admin only** — Managers see call-offs on the **Call-Offs** screen (**Today** and
> **Unacknowledged** tabs), acknowledge them, and can manually add a call-off that came in by phone
> or text.
